About the Project

What is SubAlloc Hub?

A centralized platform for managing faculty absences and substitute assignments.

When a faculty member is absent, SubAlloc Hub identifies the classes affected by the absence and searches for an available faculty member. The system checks attendance status, timetable conflicts and daily substitution workload before creating an allocation.

The system also prevents duplicate allocations, generates notifications for substitute faculty, and supports the complete workflow from assignment to acknowledgement and completion.
